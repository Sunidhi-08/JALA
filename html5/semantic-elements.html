HTML4 patterns and HTML5 meaning

Older pages often used generic containers with IDs such as <div id="header">, <div id="content">, and <div id="footer">. HTML5 introduced semantic header, main, article, and footer elements so those roles are explicit. A menu was historically treated much like a list; navigation links are now commonly grouped in nav. The distinction is meaning and accessibility, not an automatic visual style.
